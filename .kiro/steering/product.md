# Product Constitution — Silk Spider Solitaire

These decisions are permanent. Do not change them without explicit owner approval.

## Identity

- **Name**: Silk Spider Solitaire
- **Publisher**: Green Midori (studio name TBD — see STUDIO.md)
- **Package**: com.greenmidori.silkspider
- **Genre**: Single-player offline card game (Spider Solitaire)
- **Audience**: Adults 25-65+ who enjoy calm, strategic card games

## Non-Negotiable Principles

1. **No advertisements** — ever, in any form
2. **No compulsory registration** — the game works fully without an account
3. **Gameplay works offline** — internet only for purchases/restore
4. **No subscriptions** — one-time purchases only
5. **No gambling-style rewards** — no loot boxes, no random paid outcomes
6. **Free version remains genuinely playable** — 1+2 suit, daily challenge, full undo/hints
7. **Full Weaver is permanent** — a one-time purchase that never expires
8. **Purchases never improve odds of winning** — cosmetics only
9. **Original artwork and sound only** — no copied assets from Microsoft or others
10. **Player data stays on device** — no cloud telemetry, no personal data collection

## Revenue Model

| Tier | Price | What it unlocks |
|------|-------|-----------------|
| Free Weaver | $0 | 1+2 suit, daily challenge, basic stats, 3 card backs |
| Full Weaver | $3.99 | 4-suit, challenge cards, mastery, gallery, all cosmetics |
| Cosmetic Packs | $1.49-$1.99 | Visual customization (available to all) |
| Supporter Packs | $0.99-$4.99 | Guaranteed cosmetics + badges (available to all) |

## What This Game Is

- A calm, beautiful Spider Solitaire
- A game of quiet strategy and pattern recognition
- Respectful of the player's time and attention
- Accessible to players with varying abilities
- A premium experience without premium pressure

## What This Game Is NOT

- Not a gacha/collection game
- Not a social network
- Not a competitive multiplayer game
- Not a time-waster with artificial friction
- Not an ad delivery vehicle
