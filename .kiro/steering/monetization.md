# Monetization Rules — Silk Spider Solitaire

## Core Principles

1. The free version must be a **complete, enjoyable game** — not a trial
2. All purchases are **clearly priced** — no obfuscation with virtual currencies for purchase
3. **No randomized paid rewards** — every purchase guarantees specific items
4. **Cosmetics only** — purchases never affect gameplay, odds, or difficulty
5. **One-time purchases only** — no subscriptions, no expiring content
6. Upgrade prompt appears **after 5 completed games** — never on first launch

These features are **permanently free** and must never be gated:

## Forbidden Practices

- ❌ Ads of any kind
- ❌ Subscriptions
- ❌ Loot boxes / random paid rewards
- ❌ Pay-to-win mechanics
- ❌ Artificial difficulty to force purchases
- ❌ Dark patterns (confusing cancel flows, hidden charges)
- ❌ Nagging/spamming purchase prompts
- ❌ Requiring purchase to access saved games
