# Privacy Policy — Silk Spider Solitaire

**Last updated:** August 9, 2026

## Overview

Silk Spider Solitaire ("the App") is developed by Green Midori. This privacy policy explains how the App handles your information.

**The short version: We collect nothing. Your data stays on your device.**

## Data Collection

The App does **not** collect, transmit, or store any personal data. Specifically:

- **No personal information** is collected (name, email, phone, location, etc.)
- **No usage analytics** or telemetry is transmitted
- **No crash reports** are sent to any server
- **No advertising identifiers** are used
- **No cookies or tracking technologies** are employed
- **No third-party SDKs** that collect data are included

## Data Storage

All game data (saved games, statistics, preferences, daily challenge progress) is stored locally on your device using standard app storage. This data:

- Never leaves your device
- Is not backed up to any cloud service by the App
- Is deleted if you uninstall the App or clear app data

## Internet Access

The App functions entirely offline for standard gameplay. An internet connection is required **only** to:

- Make optional in-app purchases via Google Play
- Restore previously purchased items

No gameplay data, personal information, or usage statistics are transmitted over the internet. The App does not communicate with any server other than the Google Play billing service during purchase transactions.

## Permissions

The App requests **no device permissions**. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, storage, location, or any other device capabilities beyond its own sandboxed app storage.

## Third-Party Services

The App uses **no** third-party services, including:

- No advertising networks
- No analytics platforms
- No social media integrations
- No cloud storage services
- No authentication providers

## Children's Privacy

The App does not collect data from anyone, including children. It contains no advertisements, in-app purchases, links to external websites, or social features. It is suitable for all ages.

## In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases processed through Google Play Billing:

- **Full Weaver** — a one-time purchase that unlocks premium features
- **Cosmetic packs** — optional visual customization items
- **Supporter Packs** — optional purchases that include guaranteed cosmetic items

All purchases are clearly priced with no hidden costs, no subscriptions, no loot boxes, and no random paid rewards. The core game is enjoyable without any purchase. Purchase records are managed entirely by Google Play and are not stored on any external server.

## Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, the updated version will be made available at the same URL. Since the App collects no data, meaningful changes to this policy are unlikely.
