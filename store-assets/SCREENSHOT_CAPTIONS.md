## Capture Tips

1. Use a **clean device** (hide notification icons, set to 100% battery)
2. Set time to **9:41** (traditional screenshot time) or leave natural
3. Use **1080×1920** resolution minimum
4. Ensure text is **readable at thumbnail size** (Play Store shows small)
5. Keep captions to **5 words max** — they must be scannable
6. Use consistent framing across all 8
