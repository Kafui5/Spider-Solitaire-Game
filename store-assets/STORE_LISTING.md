## Full Description (4000 characters max)

Silk Spider Solitaire is a beautifully crafted Spider Solitaire game designed for quiet moments of strategic thinking. No ads, no subscriptions, no internet required — just you and the cards.

🕷️ CLASSIC SPIDER SOLITAIRE
Build eight complete runs from King to Ace. Stack cards in descending order, manage your stock deals wisely, and clear the entire tableau to win.

🎯 THREE DIFFICULTY LEVELS
• Calm (1 Suit) — A gentle introduction to Spider
• Clever (2 Suits) — Balanced strategic challenge
• Master (4 Suits) — The full, unforgiving experience

☀️ DAILY CHALLENGE
Play the same deal as every other player worldwide. Complete it to build your streak. Difficulty rotates throughout the week so every day feels fresh.

✨ POLISHED EXPERIENCE
• Smooth drag-and-drop card movement
• Satisfying animations and haptic feedback
• Intelligent hint system when you're stuck
• Unlimited undo — experiment freely
• Auto-save your game — pick up right where you left off
• Timer and move counter to track your improvement

📊 STATISTICS
Track your wins, streaks, best times, and improvement across all difficulty levels.

🎴 CUSTOMIZABLE CARD BACKS
Choose from five unique designs: Woven, Spider, Geometric, Midnight, and Royal.

🎨 ORIGINAL VISUAL DESIGN
A distinctive dark green and gold aesthetic inspired by woven textiles and silk craft. Spider web motifs and warm accent colors create a calm, focused atmosphere unlike any other solitaire app.

🔒 COMPLETELY PRIVATE
• No internet connection needed — ever
• No ads, no in-app purchases
• No accounts or sign-ups
• No data collection or analytics
• No permissions required

📱 WORKS EVERYWHERE
Responsive layout adapts beautifully to phones and tablets in both portrait and landscape orientations.

Play Silk Spider Solitaire and rediscover the quiet satisfaction of a well-played game.
