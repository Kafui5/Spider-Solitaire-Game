# Silk Spider Solitaire — Help & Support

Welcome to Silk Spider Solitaire, a beautiful and calm Spider Solitaire experience inspired by West African Kente textile patterns.

---

## Getting Started

Spider Solitaire is played with two decks of cards (104 cards total) arranged into 10 columns. Your goal is to build complete sequences of cards from King down to Ace in the same suit. When a full sequence is formed, it's automatically removed from the board.

**Basic gameplay:**
1. Arrange cards in descending order within columns (King → Queen → Jack → ... → 2 → Ace)
2. You can move any face-up card (plus any cards below it in sequence) to another column
3. Same-suit sequences are easier to move — mixed-suit sequences must be moved one card at a time
4. When stuck, deal a new row of cards from the stock pile (one card per column)
5. Complete all 8 same-suit sequences (King to Ace) to win

**Tip:** Focus on uncovering face-down cards and building same-suit sequences whenever possible.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I move cards?

Tap a card to select it, then tap the destination column. Alternatively, you can drag and drop cards between columns. The game will highlight valid destinations when you select a card.

### What are the difficulty modes?

Silk Spider Solitaire offers three difficulty levels:

- **One Suit (Apprentice)** — All cards are spades. Great for learning the game. Win rate ~50%.
- **Two Suits (Journeyman)** — Cards are spades and hearts. A satisfying challenge. Win rate ~15%.
- **Four Suits (Master)** — All four suits in play. The ultimate challenge. Win rate ~5%. Requires Full Weaver after 3 free trials.

### How does the Daily Loom work?

Each day, a new Daily Challenge is generated with a specific seed and difficulty. Every player worldwide receives the same deal. Complete it to build your streak! The difficulty rotates day by day. Your streak resets if you miss a day.

### How do I undo a move?

Tap the ↩️ undo button in the toolbar. Undo is unlimited and free for all players. You can undo all the way back to the start of the game.

### How does Auto-complete work?

When the game detects that all remaining moves will inevitably complete the game (no face-down cards remain and all sequences can be resolved), it offers to auto-complete the game with a celebratory animation. You can always decline and finish manually.

### Is an internet connection required?

No. Silk Spider Solitaire works completely offline. An internet connection is only needed for:
- Purchasing products from the Google Play Store
- Restoring purchases on a new device

All game progress, statistics, and unlocked items are stored locally on your device.

### How do I restore my purchase?

1. Open the game
2. Go to **Settings** (gear icon)
3. Tap **Restore Purchases**
4. Make sure you're signed in with the same Google account you used to purchase

Your purchases will be restored automatically. If you're having trouble, see the Troubleshooting section below.

### I bought Full Weaver but it's not unlocked?

This is usually a sync issue. Try the following:

1. Make sure you're connected to the internet
2. Go to Settings → Restore Purchases
3. Ensure you're signed into the same Google account that made the purchase
4. Restart the app
5. If the issue persists, check your Google Play purchase history to confirm the transaction completed

If none of these work, please contact support with your order ID.

### How do I reset my progress?

Go to **Settings** → scroll to the bottom → tap **Reset All Progress**. This will erase all statistics, streaks, achievements, and mastery progress. This action cannot be undone. Your purchases (Full Weaver, Supporter Packs, etc.) are NOT affected by a reset.

### Is my data collected?

No. Silk Spider Solitaire does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. There are no analytics, no tracking, no ads, and no accounts. All your game data stays on your device. See our [Privacy Policy](https://greenmidori.com/privacy) for full details.

## Troubleshooting

### Game won't start

Try these steps in order:

1. **Force-close and reopen** the app
2. **Restart your device**
3. **Check for updates** in the Google Play Store
4. **Clear app cache**: Settings → Apps → Silk Spider Solitaire → Clear Cache (this won't delete your progress)
5. **Reinstall**: Uninstall and reinstall from the Play Store. Your purchases can be restored, but local progress will be lost.

If the issue persists, please contact support with your device model and Android version.

### Purchase not going through

1. **Check your internet connection** — purchases require connectivity
2. **Verify your payment method** is valid in Google Play
3. **Restart the Google Play Store app**
4. **Clear Google Play Services cache**: Settings → Apps → Google Play Services → Clear Cache
5. **Wait and retry** — sometimes Google Play has temporary issues

If you were charged but didn't receive the item, go to Settings → Restore Purchases. If that doesn't work, contact support with your order ID from your Google Play purchase history.

### Game progress lost

Game progress is stored locally on your device. Progress can be lost if:

- The app is uninstalled
- App data is cleared (not just cache)
- The device is factory reset

Unfortunately, local progress cannot be recovered once lost. However:
- Your **purchases** (Full Weaver, packs) can always be restored
- Your **Daily Challenge streak** will restart from zero

We recommend taking screenshots of meaningful milestones. Cloud save may be added in a future update.
